What the portal covers

The system combines service requests, asset tracking, access control, and reporting in a simpler internal workspace.

  • Ticketing and updates Employees can submit issues and receive automatic status notifications without manual follow-up.
  • Assets and licenses IT can track devices by employee and location while monitoring license usage and expiration dates.
  • Visibility and accountability Managers and support staff get dashboards, audit logs, and role-based access to the right information.
